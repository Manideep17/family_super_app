## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
FAM is a private, invite-only hub built for one audience: your family.

No public feed, no strangers, no ads — just the people you actually live life
with, in one app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Family chat — a group chat just for your household.
• Shared diary — capture memories, tag who was there, react to each other's entries.
• Tasks — assign chores and to-dos, track who's on point, award points when done.
• Calendar — one shared calendar for birthdays, appointments, and family plans.
• Vault — a shared photo/video album with on-device text search (find that report
  card or bill photo instantly — the scanning happens entirely on your phone).
• Games — quizzes and prediction games built from your own family's memories.
• Leaderboard & streaks — light, optional gamification that makes showing up for
  your family a little more fun, not a competition with strangers.

BUILT FOR INDIAN FAMILIES
Festival greetings, multi-generational households, and the reality that "family"
often means parents, kids, and grandparents all trying to stay on the same page —
FAM is designed around how families actually coordinate, not a generic productivity
app repurposed for personal use.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Every family's data is walled off from every other family — enforced at the
database level, not just in the UI. There's no advertising in this app and no
ad SDKs collecting data in the background. Full details in our privacy policy,
linked from the app and the Play Store listing.

FAM is currently in active development — features are added based on real
feedback from families using it daily.
```
