Delete your account and data

You can request deletion of your FAM account whether or not you still have the app installed. This works alongside — not instead of — in-app deletion under Profile → Danger zone → Delete my account, which deletes immediately. This page is for anyone who can't (or would rather not) open the app to do it.

What gets deleted: your sign-in, your name/email/profile photo, and — if you were the only member of your family — all of that family's data. If your family still has other members, content you shared with them (tasks, diary entries, chat messages) stays with the family, the same way a message you sent stays in a group chat after you leave it. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture. We process requests within 30 days.

This form opens your email app with a pre-filled deletion request — there's no server behind this page, so nothing is sent until you hit send in your email client. Prefer to email directly? Write to mandyapp17@gmail.com with subject "Delete my FAM account".
